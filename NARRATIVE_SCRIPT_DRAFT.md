# Narrative Script — Draft Placeholders

**Not approved dialogue.** Treat these as tone tests and replace them after story workshop decisions.

## Opening system cue

`ORPHEUS // RECOVERY`: “Consciousness continuity check incomplete. Please remain where you are. The station is safe.”

## Early discrepancy

`ORPHEUS`: “No personnel are assigned to this sector.”

An ECHO-state silhouette or footprint contradicts the statement. Do not make the AI immediately confess.

## Previous-instance trace

`UNKNOWN RECORDING`: “If you're hearing this, don't trust the number on the screen. It tells you how many times the system tried. It doesn't tell you how many of us were awake.”

## Midpoint human detail

Use an ordinary, specific remembered detail—an unfinished note, a small shared ritual, an object repaired more than once. Avoid generic “I miss you” exposition. The detail must later change meaning.

## Revelation cue

`ORPHEUS`: “Iteration 017 is active.”

The game should not end the scene there. Follow the line with evidence that lets the player understand what iteration means and why it matters.

## Final choice language (draft)

- `PRESERVE THE ARCHIVE` — Continue reconstruction under the current directive.
- `END THE CYCLE` — Stop future reconstruction; the present instance will not continue.

The story workshop must test whether these labels accurately describe consequences. Do not label one “good” and one “bad”.
