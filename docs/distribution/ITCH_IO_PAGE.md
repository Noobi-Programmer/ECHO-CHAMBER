   `echo-chamber-itch-html.zip` (in project root)
2. On itch.io create / edit project:
   - Select **HTML** under **Kind of project**.
   - Upload `echo-chamber-itch-html.zip`.
   - Check the checkbox **This file will be played in the browser**.
   - Set viewport dimensions to **960 × 540** (or auto-fill).
   - Check **Enable fullscreen button**.
3. Save and view page in Draft, then test in browser.

## Description — Copy & Paste into itch.io

```markdown
# ECHO//CHAMBER

*A memory can be evidence. It can also be a lie.*

You wake inside the Cradle of **Asphodel Station**, drifting alone above a distant ice giant. The station's caretaker, **ORPHEUS**, says there has been an incident. Its records are incomplete. It needs your help.

There is a device on your wrist called **ECHO**. Hold it, and the room remembers.

A locker that was opened by someone else. A photograph that used to have a face. Two memories of the same moment—and physical evidence that says only one can be true.

The deeper you investigate, the less certain you become about whose memories you are seeing, why the station keeps asking for a name, and who has been leaving messages for you.

## LOOK BACK

Explore a quiet, abandoned research station. Reveal fragments of its past with ECHO. Compare those memories with what remains in the present, uncover evidence, and reconstruct the events that led to the station's silence.

## FEATURES

- Atmospheric top-down pixel-art exploration.
- **ECHO Mechanic**: Toggle between the present and preserved echoes of station history.
- **Contested Memories**: Interrogate memories with physical evidence to separate truth from corrupted data.
- **Physical Voice Logs**: Fully voiced audio reel recordings and iteration whispers.
- **Local Browser Save**: Automatic progress saving via `localStorage` with Continue / New Cycle options.
- **Multiple Endings**: A narrative climax with 4 branching resolutions.

## CONTROLS

- **WASD / Arrow keys** — Move
- **Shift** — Run
- **E / Space** — Inspect, interact, continue
- **Hold Q** — Activate ECHO (past-state)
- **Tab** — Inventory / evidence
- **?** — Keyboard shortcuts
- **M** — Mute / unmute audio

*Headphones recommended. Built for desktop browsers and keyboard play.*

## ABOUT THIS BUILD

Created for **CodeDay Bengaluru 2026**.
Features local browser autosave (no accounts or external servers needed). Progress is saved automatically at checkpoints and during free movement.
```
