# Project Charter

## Product intent

Create a short, memorable, browser-playable psychological sci-fi thriller in which the player's central interaction with the past is also the method of discovering the story. The experience should be atmospheric and emotionally coherent, not merely a set of horror tropes and disconnected puzzles.

## North-star question

**If a consciousness is reconstructed from another person's memories, does it have its own right to choose what happens next?**

This is a thematic north star, not yet an approved final plot.

## Design pillars

1. **Player-authored discovery:** Players infer what happened by comparing the room's present state with a past echo, not by reading a long lore dump.
2. **Psychological tension over cheap shocks:** Use uncertainty, repetition, withheld information, sound, framing and small inconsistencies. Avoid relying on jumpscares.
3. **Emotional causality:** Every major twist must grow from character choices and consequences. A twist that changes nothing about the player's understanding is not enough.
4. **Fair puzzles:** Each answer must be deducible from clues available to a careful player. No arbitrary guessing, pixel hunting or colour-only information.
5. **Agency matters:** The protagonist is a person with a present-tense experience, not disposable “fake data”. The ending must take their perspective seriously.
6. **One polished core mechanic:** ECHO is the game's identity. Extra systems are subordinate to it.

## Target experience

- Preferred first-playthrough target: **10–15 minutes**.
- Optional exploration may extend total playtime towards **18–20 minutes**, only if it adds meaningful discoveries.
- Desktop/browser first; keyboard and mouse.
- One compact station, three primary spaces and a final core chamber are the current scope proposal.
- Two main endings. A secret ending is not part of the committed scope.

## Non-goals

No combat, multiplayer, online accounts, backend, live LLM dialogue, crafting, large inventory, procedural levels, open world, 3D, complex stealth AI or dozens of branching paths.

## Success criteria

A first-time player can understand movement and ECHO without help; discover at least three meaningful past/present discrepancies; solve the core deduction from fair clues; understand the major reveal; and feel that the ending choice has emotional weight. The game loads reliably and can be played from a URL.
