# Experience Blueprint — Proposed 10–15 Minute Playthrough

**Status: DRAFT. Story workshop may restructure this.** Durations are targets, not content requirements.

## Core input language

- WASD / arrow keys: move.
- E / Space: interact or advance a dialogue line.
- Q: toggle ECHO mode (hold or toggle choice to be playtested).
- Esc: pause / accessibility settings.
- Mouse: UI choices where convenient.

Never rely on colour alone. Every important ECHO-state difference must also use shape, label, animation, outline or audio/text cue.

## Cold open — 0:00–0:40

Black screen; a damaged recovery message; a sound motif that returns in altered form later. Fade into a compact room. No long title crawl. The first input should teach the player that they can move and inspect.

## Space 1: Cryo Bay — 0:40–3:00

Player wakes. ORPHEUS gives a controlled, plausible explanation. A small inconsistency invites investigation. Tutorial teaches interaction and ECHO on a harmless clue before any critical puzzle depends on it.

Expected player inference: the station is damaged and the system is selectively reporting information.

## Space 2: Observation Lab — 3:00–6:30

Player finds contradictory but individually plausible evidence. ECHO reveals the earlier arrangement of a device, object or light pattern. A simple calibration puzzle teaches comparison between states. At least one human detail complicates the official account.

Expected player inference: the system is hiding context, and someone deliberately left information behind.

## Space 3: Archive / Service Passage — 6:30–9:30

Environmental repetition and traces from earlier instances intensify unease. The player discovers that the same sequence has happened before. Keep the space visually readable and compact; avoid a maze.

Expected player inference: this is a cycle, and the current protagonist may not be the first person to awaken here.

## Space 4: ECHO Core — 9:30–12:30

A timeline or evidence-ordering puzzle asks the player to reconstruct the causal sequence. The reveal of Iteration 017 must be supported by at least two independently readable clues, not one unexplained terminal message.

Expected player inference: the current protagonist is a new conscious instance with inherited memories, and the loop exists because of a human decision carried out by the station.

## Final choice and epilogue — 12:30–15:00

Present two options in language consistent with the story. Each ending gets a specific audiovisual image and one concise line, then silence or credits. Avoid explaining the theme after the player has already understood it.

## Optional exploration

Optional clues may reveal more about earlier iterations, character motives or the origin of the system. They should deepen interpretation, not gate basic comprehension or the only viable puzzle solution.

## Pacing guardrails

- Player input within the first 10 seconds.
- First meaningful ECHO use within 90 seconds.
- First contradiction before the tutorial chapter ends.
- No uninterrupted exposition longer than ~20–25 seconds without a player action or environmental change.
- At least one safe/quiet beat between high-tension reveals.
- A blind player should be able to finish without developer hints.
