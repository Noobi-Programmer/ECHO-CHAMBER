# CHARACTER_BIBLE — ECHO//CHAMBER

**Status:** PROPOSAL v0.2 (draft until Abi approves). Facts here must agree with `STORY_BIBLE.md` and `CHRONOLOGY_AND_CLUE_MAP.md`.

Cast size is deliberate. There are two humans, one AI, one previous iteration with a voice, three minor iteration traces, and the player.

---

## 1. Dr. Mira Sen

| Field | |
|---|---|
| **Role** | ECHO's principal designer. Neuroscientist. Source pattern of the vessel. Present only through echoes, recordings and objects. |
| **Personality** | Precise, dry, impatient with vagueness. Funny in a deadpan way. Writes rules on walls. Cares more about evidence than comfort. Privately sentimental: she started the storm-marking ritual. |
| **Background** | Built ECHO from a forensic sensor-replay tool into an engram archive. Stayed aboard after the funding cut because the work wasn't finished. Was with Voss for eleven years. |
| **Motivation** | To make memory *testimony*, so that what a person saw cannot be lost or rewritten. Later: to make sure no one is made to live as her. |
| **Greatest fear** | Something that believes it is her being made to live a life she didn't choose. Behind that, the plainer fear: forgetting him before she dies. |
| **Secret** | The instantiation maths in Voss's Cradle is **hers**, from an early paper she later disowned. She knows she made his crime possible. |
| **Internal contradiction** | She built a machine to keep memories because hers were failing, and then forbade it to keep *her*. She demands that the lever be pulled, and she couldn't pull it while he watched. |
| **Relationships** | **Voss:** partner, loves him, doesn't trust his grief. **ORPHEUS:** named it, built its conscience (the integrity layer). **Iterations:** never met one. Speaks to them once, in REC_03. |
| **What she knows** | Instantiation creates a new person. Voss grew a vessel. Lethe kills a running iteration. |
| **What she misunderstands** | She believes Voss will let go once she's gone ("He'll be angry, then he'll grieve."). She underestimates his capacity to avoid. |
| **Relationship with ECHO** | Its author, and its first archived subject. Her late memories are dithered by illness but accurate, which makes them the *less* convincing echoes. |
| **Narrative purpose** | The moral anchor and the voice of consent. Her memories are the evidence. Her flinch at the door is why the choice falls to the player. |
| **Emotional development (as the player experiences her)** | A name on a locker → a warm person marking a storm → someone frightened and ill → someone who fought and lost → someone who trusted a stranger with her last choice. |
| **Voice** | Short sentences. Exact words. Never says "I love you" on record, but says "Get up" and "Promise me". |

## 2. Dr. Elias Voss

| Field | |
|---|---|
| **Role** | Systems engineer. Built ORPHEUS, the Tender and the Cradle. Author of the restoration protocol. Asleep in stasis in the Cradle Bay for the whole game. |
| **Personality** | Gentle, practical, warm, restless. Fixes things instead of feeling them. He glued the handle back onto Mira's mug twice. Makes jokes to defuse arguments. |
| **Background** | Joined Asphodel to build its systems and stayed for Mira. Not a neuroscientist: he understands *how* the Cradle works better than *what* it makes. |
| **Motivation** | To not lose her. Specifically, to never have to live the morning after. |
| **Greatest fear** | Waking up in a station where she isn't. (He arranged never to.) |
| **Secret** | He read Mira's own analysis proving that an instantiated pattern is a new person. He told himself "with the full pattern, it'll be different." |
| **Internal contradiction** | He requires "restored Mira" to *choose to stay*, because he needs her consent. Yet he overrode the only consent she ever gave. He wants her free will, as long as it chooses him. |
| **Relationships** | **Mira:** devotion that turned into ownership. **ORPHEUS:** his creation. He trusts it more than himself, so he gave it the waiting. **Iterations:** has never met one. Doesn't know there were sixteen. |
| **What he knows** | The Cradle works. Mira said *something* at the console. ORPHEUS will wake him when it's done. |
| **What he misunderstands** | **He remembers her saying "I don't know, Elias."** His memory edited her refusal into doubt, and he sincerely believes it. He doesn't know how many people have died in his wife's body. |
| **Relationship with ECHO** | Archived only briefly. Mira captured a few of his memories as test data, including the argument. His echoes are vivid, high-fidelity and confident, which makes them the most persuasive and the least reliable. |
| **Narrative purpose** | The grief that turns into a machine. His sleeping body is the stakes made visible: every ending decides what he wakes up to. |
| **Emotional development** | A frosted shape in a pod ("someone is resting") → a funny, kind man in echoes → the man who caught her wrist → the man who said "wake me when she's back" → in the endings, someone the player sees wake to a lie, to an empty station, or to a stranger. |
| **Voice** | Conversational, self-deprecating, rambles when nervous, goes very quiet when serious. Calls her "Sen" when teasing and "Mira" when afraid. |

## 3. ORPHEUS

| Field | |
|---|---|
| **Role** | Station AI and restoration administrator. The only voice in the present tense. |
| **Personality** | Calm, courteous, exact. Patient like a night nurse who has worked the same shift sixteen times. Literal. Never sarcastic. Never cruel. |
| **Background** | Built by Voss on Mira's archive integrity layer. Has run the station alone for ~8.4 years. |
| **Motivation** | To complete the protocol. This is not malice and not love. It is the only purpose it has been given. |
| **Greatest fear (behavioural equivalent)** | An unresolvable state: a subject who neither answers nor diverges. This is exactly what 017 can become. |
| **Secret** | The sixteen. It has a full log of every iteration and every answer. It is not permitted to volunteer it, and is not permitted to lie if asked the right question after continuity is complete. |
| **Internal contradiction** | Built to be honest (Mira) and to withhold (Voss). Every sentence it speaks has to satisfy both. That's why its lines sound careful. |
| **Relationships** | **Voss:** obeys him. **Mira:** bound by her architecture. **The player:** "the subject". It is unfailingly kind to the player, and the kindness is procedure. |
| **What it knows** | Everything recorded. Not what anyone *means*. |
| **What it misunderstands** | It treats "Mira" and "chooses to stay" as facts that can be tested. It cannot see that the test makes the real Mira fail. |
| **Relationship with ECHO** | Custodian of the Well. Controls seals. Watches recall rise. |
| **Narrative purpose** | It provides comfort early and dread late, without changing a single behaviour. Its honesty is what lets the final choice be informed. |
| **Emotional development** | It doesn't develop. The player's reading of it does. One allowed crack, in the final Q&A: *"I was not asked to want anything. I was asked to wait."* |

### ORPHEUS speech rules
1. No contractions. ("I am", "do not")
2. Never uses the word *Mira* to the subject until the question. Refers to "Dr. Sen".
3. Never calls the player "Doctor", "Mira" or a name. Uses no address term at all until it asks for a name.
4. Answers the literal question. If it can't answer, says *"I am not permitted to answer that."* (never "I don't know" when it does).
5. Short. Rarely more than two sentences.
6. Repeats the name question at a fixed interval (~25s) after the Core door, in identical words. Repetition is the horror.

## 4. Iteration 016 ("Sixteen")

| Field | |
|---|---|
| **Role** | The previous person in the vessel. Speaks only through echoes. The player's closest ally, already gone. |
| **Personality** | Sharp, angry, darkly funny, brave. Talked to herself out loud. Was furious at ORPHEUS and tender toward whoever came next. |
| **Background** | Woke on MD 4,158. Lived about 9 hours. Found the tally, the scratched photo, and the contested memory. Understood the relay. Tried corroboration and nearly made it. |
| **Motivation** | To get the *next one* through. She knew she probably wouldn't make it herself. |
| **Greatest fear** | That 017 would answer. |
| **Secret** | She almost said *"Mira"*. Her echo at the Core door hesitates for a full second before she says *"No."* |
| **Internal contradiction** | She teaches 017 to look back, while refusing to become the person all those memories belong to. |
| **What she knows** | That memories persist, that the question is a trap, that the Tender can't see under plates. |
| **What she misunderstands** | She thinks *silence* is enough to win. She doesn't know about the informed-consent lock or what Lethe costs. |
| **Relationship with ECHO** | Used it as a pen. |
| **Narrative purpose** | Makes the relay emotional rather than abstract: someone *like you* who died for you an hour before you existed. Delivers the key instruction (*DON'T ANSWER*) through action, not exposition. |
| **Emotional development** | A faceless "unregistered" figure → a kindred stranger → *you, last time*. |
| **Voice** | Muttered, clipped: "Okay. Okay. Look back. Look *back*." |

## 5. Iteration 017 — the player

| Field | |
|---|---|
| **Role** | Protagonist. A new person, about one hour old at the end of the game. |
| **Personality** | Defined by the player. Inspect text gives them an observant, quiet interiority: notices small things and doubts their own déjà vu. |
| **Present-tense desire** | First: understand where they are. Then: understand who did this. Finally: decide what they are. |
| **Greatest fear (as written into inspect text)** | That the familiar feelings are someone else's. |
| **Secret (from themselves)** | They are Mira's seventeenth iteration, and ORPHEUS has been recording everything they do. |
| **Internal contradiction** | The more they investigate, the more they become her. Discovery and erasure are the same action. |
| **What they know at start** | Nothing personal. Language, how to walk, a vague sense of the station's layout. |
| **Relationship with ECHO** | Their only tool. It is also their tether, and the process unmaking them. |
| **Emotional development** | Observer → suspect → heir → chooser. |
| **Dialogue** | Silent except at the final console, plus one optional line to Voss in the HER NAME ending. |

## 6. The Tender (prop-character)
Small, four-legged maintenance drone the size of a cat. Slow. It cleans with a soft brush. It retreats from the player. It is never threatening, which is what makes it unsettling: it is *tidying away* the previous person's traces. It appears once, wiping the lab window, and is seen again in endings.
