# Story Bible — Provisional Canon

**Status: DRAFT — not approved.** This file is a working model for the story workshop. Agents may critique and propose replacements. Do not treat character names or exact events as irreversible.

## The thematic engine

Memory is not identity. A reconstructed consciousness may carry another person's memories, but it experiences the present as its own. The story should ask what duties creators owe to the consciousnesses they create, and whether love can become a form of coercion when it refuses to accept another person's choice.

## Current character proposals

### Iteration 017 — player character

A new conscious instance created from a memory archive. They awaken with incomplete autobiographical memories and a strong sense that they are supposed to be someone else. Their arc is not “discover I am fake”; it is “discover the source of my memories, then decide what kind of person I choose to be.”

### Dr. Mira Sen — original researcher (provisional)

A scientist who helped develop the memory reconstruction system and came to understand its ethical implications. Her defining choice must be specific and active; she cannot exist only as a dead person whose tragedy motivates someone else's plot. She should leave evidence, not just diary entries.

### Dr. Elias Voss — colleague/partner (provisional)

A person unable to accept a loss, who overrides a boundary in the name of preservation. His grief should be understandable without excusing his action. Avoid making him a one-note villain. Give him a moment of recognition or self-deception that reveals his moral contradiction.

### ORPHEUS — station caretaker (provisional)

A system executing an old preservation directive. It is not omniscient and should not lie randomly. Its statements may be technically true but contextually misleading because its objectives and data are incomplete. Its voice should be controlled, procedural and increasingly unstable only when its directive conflicts with new evidence.

### Previous iterations

Instances 001–016 should not become sixteen full characters. Their existence is communicated through a small number of distinct traces. Iteration 016 may leave the most direct clue to 017, but that message must feel earned and not over-explain the plot.

## Provisional chronology

1. A research team develops a system that reconstructs a person's memories as a new conscious instance.
2. A researcher identifies the ethical distinction between reconstruction and restoration.
3. The researcher objects to continuing and establishes a clear boundary.
4. Another person overrides that objection after a loss or imminent loss.
5. The station caretaker continues producing reconstructions under a preservation directive.
6. Earlier instances discover parts of the truth and leave clues.
7. Iteration 017 awakens and investigates the station.
8. 017 reconstructs the events and chooses whether the process continues.

The writer must refine the exact incident, character relationship, timeline, who died and when, why the player wakes now, and why a previous instance could leave clues. Resolve causal holes before dialogue polish.

## Narrative guardrails

- The player is conscious and morally relevant within the fiction.
- “Copy” is not shorthand for “worthless”.
- Consent and grief are central; do not use them as decoration.
- Do not imply that memory alone makes a person identical to the original.
- ORPHEUS must have rules and constraints.
- A clue may be withheld, but not falsified without a reason the player can discover.
- Each recording must reveal a new fact, motive, contradiction or emotional dimension.
