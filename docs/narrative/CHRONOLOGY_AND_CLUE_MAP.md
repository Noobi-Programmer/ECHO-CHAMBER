# CHRONOLOGY_AND_CLUE_MAP — ECHO//CHAMBER

**Status:** PROPOSAL v0.2. This is the **fact ledger**. If any other document disagrees with §1, this one wins until Abi rules otherwise.

Dates use **MD** (Mission Day, counted from Asphodel's commissioning). Players never need exact MD numbers. A few appear on terminals for texture and for the timeline puzzle.

- Voss has been in stasis **3,077 days ≈ 8.4 years**.

## 4. Evidence map (per major revelation)

### R1 — "The figures in ECHO are previous iterations, not crew"
| | |
|---|---|
| **Initial assumption** | The ghost hiding the card is Mira or Voss: "the past" means the researchers. |
| **Evidence** | Opening memory (figure kneels at the plate, says "look back"). The figure is **faceless**, and by rule E12 the faceless figure is the one remembering. Mira is faceless in her own memories but has a face in Voss's, which teaches the rule. The figure wears a pale gown identical to the player's. `SOURCE: — UNREGISTERED —` (not M.SEN or E.VOSS). It hides the card *from ORPHEUS*, which the crew wouldn't need to do. Single dots in the storm echo after Mira's last. 016 at the Core door. |
| **Misleading interpretation** | "Unregistered" = a crew member ORPHEUS forgot, or a third survivor. |
| **Actual explanation** | Previous iterations' memories are archived. |
| **Earliest inference** | End of Ch1 for attentive players (the gown). Most players will get it in Ch2 (storm dots). |
| **Confirmation** | Timeline puzzle: the fifth slot resolves `SOURCE: R-017`. Log lists R-001…R-016. |

### R2 — "You are a new person grown from Mira's pattern"
| | |
|---|---|
| **Initial assumption** | You are a crew member or technician recovering from stasis. |
| **Evidence** | ORPHEUS asks your name and says "It will come back." The mug inspect ("You don't know how you know"). Palm locker ("It recognised you."). Recall % on terminals. The photo echo shows Mira's face. REC_03 "whatever wakes up with my memories". |
| **Misleading interpretation** | "I *am* Mira, with amnesia." This is the intended midpoint suspicion. |
| **Actual explanation** | Woven pattern in a vessel. A new person. |
| **Earliest inference** | During REC_03 (midpoint). |
| **Confirmation** | Log header "SUBJECT PATTERN: M. SEN — VESSEL 1 — WEAVE 17". Reflection close-up. |

### R3 — "Your investigation was the restoration"
| | |
|---|---|
| **Initial assumption** | ORPHEUS is helping you investigate an incident. |
| **Evidence** | ORPHEUS's opening task ("help me restore the station's records"). Recall % rises with each discovery. ORPHEUS: "Your recall is improving." P-3 quoted in REC_04 ("let her find her way back… let her look"). Completing the timeline triggers *"Continuity restored. Recall 94%."* |

### R4 — "The test can only be passed by betraying Mira"

## 5. Red herrings (fair, and each resolves)

| Red herring | Why it's believable | How it resolves |
|---|---|---|
| **Voss is the villain who killed Mira.** | He stopped her physically. He's "hiding" in a pod. | REC_04 and the medbay reference: she died of illness. His crime is the override, not murder. |
| **Voss's vivid memory is the truth.** | It's the crispest echo in the game. | Physical evidence (snapped key) supports Mira's dithered memory. *Clarity is not truth.* |
| **ORPHEUS is lying to you.** | It stonewalls and withholds. | It never says anything false. The `NARRATIVE_SCRIPT.md` line audit proves it. |
| **"Unregistered" figure = surviving crew member.** | The crew photo shows six people. | Gown, tally, storm dots, log. |
| **You are Mira with amnesia.** | Locker, mug, face. | REC_03 + log: not amnesia, a weave. |
