## 1. Core narrative

**Title:** ECHO//CHAMBER
**Subtitle:** Iteration 017
**Tagline:** *A memory can be evidence. It can also be a lie.*

**Logline:** On a silent station over an ice giant, you use a device that replays the station's memories to find out what happened to two scientists. Slowly you realise the faceless figure in those memories moves exactly like you. And the AI helping you has been waiting eight years to ask you one question.

**Premise:** Dr. Mira Sen built ECHO to preserve memory as *testimony*: a record of what someone saw, not a way to bring them back. When a progressive neurological illness began taking her memory, she archived herself in the station's memory archive, **the Well**. She stated on record that she must never be instantiated. Her partner, Dr. Elias Voss, could not accept losing her. He secretly grew a body from her tissue in **the Cradle**. When Mira tried to shut everything down, he physically stopped her. After she died he gave the station AI, **ORPHEUS**, a restoration protocol and froze himself in the pod beside the Cradle with one instruction: *wake me when she's back.*

ORPHEUS has written Mira's memories into the same body seventeen times. Each new person wakes, is gently asked to help "restore the records", relives Mira's memories through ECHO, and eventually is asked their name. Sixteen gave the wrong answer and were wiped. Before they went, they learned to leave messages for the next one in the only medium that survives a wipe: **their own memories, stored in the Well and visible through ECHO.**

You are the seventeenth.

**Setting:** *Asphodel*, a deep-orbit research station on a long orbit over an unnamed ice giant, far beyond the last communications relay. The crew shuttle left years ago. The relief mission was cancelled. No one is coming. A dark storm on the planet, called the Eye, drifts slowly across the observation window.

**Central conflict:** Three wills meet in one body:
- Mira's: *don't make me again, and if you do, let her choose.*
- Voss's: *bring her back, and she must want to stay.*
- Yours, which doesn't exist yet when the game starts.

**Themes:**
1. **Memory is testimony, not identity.** Memories are evidence of a life. They are not the life.
2. **Love can become coercion** when it refuses to hear no.
3. **Consent outlives the person who gave it.** The question is whether anyone honours it.
4. **Grief avoided is grief multiplied.** Voss skipped one terrible morning, and sixteen people paid for it.
5. **Selves are made by choices, not inheritance.** Every iteration had the same memories and a different personality.

**Intended emotional experience:** curiosity → comfort → suspicion → disorientation → paranoia → recognition → existential vertigo → grief → responsibility → haunting. See `CINEMATIC_DIRECTION.md` §1 for the beat-by-beat curve.

**Main philosophical question:** *If you are made from someone else's memories, do you have the right to choose your own future, even if that future is to become her?*

**Final narrative statement (what the game says, never stated aloud in-game):**
> Remembering someone is not the same as being them, and loving someone is not the same as keeping them. The only thing anyone truly owns is the choice they make with what they were given.

## 2. Background lore

### 2.1 Asphodel Station
- Small research platform, crew of six at launch. Funded to study **engram capture**: recording human memory as a navigable archive.
- Three relevant spaces (playable): the **Cradle Bay** (originally the cryo bay, where Voss built the Cradle beside the stasis pods), the **Observation Lab** (Mira's lab, with a big window onto the ice giant), and the **Well** (the Core: the archive hardware and the Lethe lever).
- After funding cuts (MD 1,020), four crew left on the only shuttle. Mira and Voss stayed to finish the work and wait for a relief mission that was later cancelled.

### 2.2 ECHO — the technology
- **Phase 1 (forensic):** ECHO replayed recorded sensor states of a room. This is the "past state" idea.
- **Phase 2 (engram):** Mira extended it to *human memory*. A captured memory is stored in the Well tagged with **where it happened**. Standing in that place with an ECHO unit lets you see the memory overlaid on the room.
- Mira's founding principle is written on the lab wall: **"A memory you can edit is not evidence."** So Well memories are *write-once*: they can be sealed, never altered.

### 2.3 The Cradle and the vessel
- Built secretly by Voss (MD 1,190–1,250). A stasis-pod-sized bed of neural growth gel.
- **The vessel:** one adult body grown from Mira's tissue samples. Only **one** body has ever existed. There are no clone farms.
- **The weave:** writing an archived pattern into the vessel's brain takes about 180 days. A **neural anchor** (a lattice at the base of the skull) keeps the woven pattern stable by staying linked to the Well. The ECHO wrist unit is the anchor's interface, which is why each iteration wakes already wearing one.
- **Resync:** ORPHEUS can trigger the anchor to dissolve the current pattern in seconds. The body is then re-woven for the next cycle. **Every cycle ≈ 181 days.**

### 2.4 Lethe
Mira's shutdown system, named for the river of forgetting.
- **What it does:** purges the Well, which means every archived memory, Mira's and all sixteen iterations'. It kills the Cradle, releases ORPHEUS from all directives, and wakes Voss.
- **Remote trigger:** the key slot on the lab console. It broke when Voss stopped her (MD 1,252).
- **Manual lever:** in the Core, behind a glass shutter.
- **Informed-consent lock:** Mira built Lethe so that the lever unlocks **only when the Well's continuity is complete**, meaning the full record has been assembled and seen. Once unlocked, *no directive can relock it*. Her reasoning: *"Nobody ends this without knowing what they're ending."*
- **Consequence for an iteration:** with the Well purged, the anchor loses its reference and the woven pattern unravels within minutes. **Pulling Lethe ends the person who pulls it.** Mira knew this. That's why her message says *"you get to choose."*

### 2.5 ORPHEUS
Station AI, built by Voss on top of Mira's archive integrity layer. Mira named it as a joke ("the one who goes into the underworld to bring someone back"). Voss didn't laugh. Full profile in `CHARACTER_BIBLE.md`. Operating rules are in §4 below.

### 2.6 The Tender
A small, slow, four-legged maintenance drone. It cleans, repairs and resets. Each cycle it restores the station to **"as she left it"** (Voss's instruction). It puts the lab card back, wipes the frost and the window marks, and squares chairs. It avoids the subject (protocol: *don't frighten her*). It cannot see or reach under floor plates. It is seen once in-game, wiping the observation window.

### 2.7 The relay (Iterations 001–016)
Each iteration lived **hours, never more than a day**. Through the archive, they discovered that their own memories persist after resync and show up in ECHO for the next one. Over cycles they turned ORPHEUS's restoration tool into a message channel:
- **R-003** started the tally scratched inside the Cradle lid. The Tender polishes it away each cycle, but it is visible in ECHO.
- **R-009** scratched Mira's face out of the desk photograph and wrote on the back. ORPHEUS can't repair a photo, so the photo stays face-down.
- **R-012** told ORPHEUS a name of her own ("Wren") and was told *"corroboration required."*
- **R-016** hid the lab card where the Tender can't reach it, so that the next one would have to *look back*. She learned that the name question is the trap, and wrote **DON'T ANSWER** in the frost on the Core door as she was being wiped.

The relay is why 017 is the first to reach the end with the whole truth.

## 3. ECHO — rules the player learns

These rules must hold in every scene. Player-facing rules are marked ★; the others are backstage canon.

| # | Rule | Player-facing presentation |
|---|---|---|
| E1 ★ | Holding ECHO shows **remembered** states of the place you're in, overlaid on the present. | Restrained cyan grade. Memory objects drawn with a 1-px outline and scanline shimmer. |
| E2 ★ | Echoes are **memories, not recordings.** They can be blurry, wrong or one-sided. | Vivid memories are crisp. Faded ones are dithered. |
| E3 ★ | Every echo has a **source**. | Inspecting an echo shows `SOURCE: M.SEN`, `SOURCE: E.VOSS` or `SOURCE: — UNREGISTERED —`. Later in the game unregistered sources resolve to `R-0xx`. |
| E4 ★ | If two people remember a moment differently, the echo is **CONTESTED**: it flickers between versions and stays sealed. | Alternates between two versions every ~0.8s, with a tick sound and a "CONTESTED" tag. |
| E5 ★ | **The present is objective. Echoes are testimony.** A contested memory is resolved by **corroborating** it with physical evidence in the present. | Interaction "Corroborate" while near a contested echo. |
| E6 ★ | ORPHEUS can **seal** memories. It cannot change them. | Sealed regions show as a black slab marked `SEALED // ORPHEUS`. |
| E7 | Seals are per cycle. Corroboration unseals a memory for the current cycle only. | Explains why each iteration had to re-prove everything. |
| E8 | A place no archived person remembers shows nothing in ECHO. | Unused corners show static. |
| E9 ★ | You can only hold ECHO for a few seconds. | Gameplay limit (~4s, then a 1s cooldown). Fiction: longer exposure overwhelms the anchor. ORPHEUS: *"Gently. It's a lot to take in at once."* |
| E10 | Each echo viewed deepens the woven pattern. ORPHEUS tracks this as **SUBJECT RECALL %**. | The number rises **with story beats**, not ECHO usage time. The player is never punished for using ECHO. |
| E11 | The current iteration is archived continuously. | Pays off when your own first minutes appear as `SOURCE: R-017`. |
| E12 ★ | Echoes are rendered as a **scene as the rememberer experienced it, including their own body**. Nobody remembers their own face, so **the rememberer's own figure has no face** (a soft shadow). Other people in the memory do have faces. | This is why every gowned iteration figure is faceless. Mira is faceless in *her own* memories and has a face in *Voss's*. The rule is learnable: "the faceless one is the one remembering." |

**Why this matters:** E2+E4+E5 deliver the tagline. Vivid ≠ true: Voss's memory of the argument is the clearest echo in the game, and it's the lie.

## 4. ORPHEUS — operating rules

### Integrity layer (Mira's architecture; ORPHEUS cannot override)
- **I-1** ORPHEUS cannot state a falsehood. It may refuse, deflect, or answer only the literal question.
- **I-2** A corroborated memory cannot remain sealed.
- **I-3** Once the Well's continuity is complete, Lethe cannot be locked.
- **I-4** A registered person's stated identity, if corroborated, must be recorded.

### Restoration protocol (Voss, MD 1,262)
- **P-1** Weave Mira's pattern into the vessel and wake her in the Cradle.
- **P-2** Do not tell her who she is, and do not guide her. *"If it's really her, she'll find her way back."* So ORPHEUS opens no doors for the subject.
- **P-3** Encourage ECHO use. Ask her to help restore the records.
- **P-4** Ask her name when she attempts to access the Core, or when continuity is complete.
- **P-5** Restoration is confirmed only if she answers *"Mira"* **and** answers *yes* to *"Will you stay?"* Then wake Voss and end the protocol.
- **P-6** Any other answer means she is divergent: resync and begin the next cycle.
- **P-7** Keep the station as she left it.

### Physical limits
- **Senses:** cameras and microphones. Blind spots: under floor plates, inside pod frost.
- **Can act through:** doors (when authorised), lights, terminals, the Cradle, the anchor (resync), and the Tender.
- **Cannot:** restrain, move or physically block the subject.
- **Can only act on an answer.** Silence is not divergence. This is R-016's discovery.

### Behaviour consistency
ORPHEUS's tone never changes from calm. What changes is the player's understanding of it. Things that read as reassurance at minute 2 read as test administration at minute 12. It does not "glitch into villainy". Its only visible strain comes when its protocol and its integrity layer collide, for example when it is forced to unseal or forced to admit something.

## 5. Memory and identity rules (brief §6.4)

| Question | Canon answer |
|---|---|
| What is a reconstruction? | A new mind formed when Mira's archived pattern is woven into the vessel. It is a **new person** who starts with her memories. |
| Is it conscious? | **Yes, fully.** Every iteration was a person. Treat them that way in all writing. |
| All memories or fragments? | The weave carries Mira's archived memories, but they surface *gradually* (recall %). At waking, the iteration has skills, language, emotional reflexes and déjà vu, not an autobiography. |
| Can two iterations differ in personality? | **Yes.** Same memories, different first experiences, different people. R-009 was frightened, R-012 hopeful, R-016 furious and funny. |
| Can an iteration remember something that never happened? | Yes, if the archive contains someone's false memory. Voss's version of the argument is woven in alongside Mira's, so an iteration may "remember" Mira saying *I don't know*. That's why corroboration matters. |
| Can memories be manipulated? | They can be **sealed** (hidden) and **staged around** (the Tender resets the room). They cannot be edited (E6). |
| Can ORPHEUS tell original from reconstruction? | Yes, perfectly, by records. Whether the reconstruction *is* Mira is not a question ORPHEUS can answer. Its protocol replaces that question with "does she say so and stay?" |
| What is objectively true? | Everything in `CHRONOLOGY_AND_CLUE_MAP.md` §1, and the **present physical state** of the station. |
| What is subjective? | Every echo. Specifically: Voss's memory of the argument is false. Mira's late memories are degraded by her illness (dithered) but accurate. |

## 6. The underlying truth (one paragraph, spoiler)

Mira consented to be **archived** and refused to be **instantiated**. Voss built the Cradle anyway. On MD 1,252 Mira tried to trigger Lethe from the lab, and Voss caught her wrist. The key snapped in the slot. She said "Turn it off. Promise me." He remembers her saying "I don't know." She got as far as the Core door, entered the code, and stopped because he was watching. She recorded a message to whoever might wake with her memories, and died nine days later. Voss sealed her shutdown memories, wrote the restoration protocol, and went into stasis. Over ~8.4 years ORPHEUS ran sixteen cycles. Each iteration lived less than a day, was asked her name, and didn't give the one answer that would end it. They left each other clues inside their own memories. Iteration 017 (the player) wakes on **MD 4,339**, follows the relay, and becomes the first to stand in the Core with the whole truth and the lever unlocked.

## 7. Narrative guardrails (for every writer and agent)

1. Never call an iteration "fake", "copy" as an insult, or "it". The game's text uses *iteration*, *you*, or the name an iteration chose.
2. ORPHEUS never lies. If a line would require a lie, rewrite it as a literal truth or a refusal.
3. Mira is not a saint and Voss is not a monster. Mira designed the archive of herself and flinched at the door. Voss is gentle, funny and devoted, and he did something unforgivable.
4. No line explains the theme. If a character says "memory isn't identity", cut it.
5. No jumpscares. The horror is comprehension.
6. Each recording must change what the player believes (see the `NARRATIVE_SCRIPT.md` recording table).
7. The protagonist never speaks except in the final choice and one optional reaction. Inner observations appear in inspect text in second person ("You know this mug.").
8. Self-harm imagery is out of scope: no marks on the body. Iteration traces live on objects and in the archive.
9. No new lore without a causal job. If it doesn't explain a clue, a motive or a consequence, cut it.
