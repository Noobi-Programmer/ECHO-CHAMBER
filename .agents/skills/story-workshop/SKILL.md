# Story Workshop

Start by reading STORY_WORKSHOP.md, STORY_BIBLE.md, EXPERIENCE_BLUEPRINT.md and STORY_DECISIONS.md. Challenge the premise before writing. Generate distinct treatments, compare them with the review rubric, and recommend one with reasons. Build a playable beat sheet: player action, information gained, belief changed, emotion, puzzle/interaction and transition. Separate objective chronology from reveal order. Keep all proposals under research/story/ until the human lead approves. Do not silently change canon.
