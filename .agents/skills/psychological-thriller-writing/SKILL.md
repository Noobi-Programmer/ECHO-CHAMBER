# Psychological Thriller Writing

Prioritize subtext, specificity, restraint and character voice. Avoid generic horror phrases, lore dumps, villain monologues and exposition that simply explains the twist. Each line must reveal motive, conceal something plausibly, alter player understanding or create tension. Use silence and environmental events where dialogue is unnecessary. Keep lines short enough for gameplay. Check all lines against the canonical knowledge map.
