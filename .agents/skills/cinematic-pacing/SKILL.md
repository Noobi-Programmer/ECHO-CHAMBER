# Cinematic Pacing

Plan tension and release across the playthrough. Prefer interactive reveals over long cutscenes. Track the first input, first clue, first anomaly, midpoint reversal, reveal and choice. Keep exposition bursts short and interleave them with exploration. Use quiet beats after major reveals. Write timing ranges, but validate through blind playtests; never mistake duration targets for player experience quality.
