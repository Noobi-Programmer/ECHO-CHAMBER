# Soundscape Design

Create an audio cue sheet linked to game beats and state changes. Define ambience, local machinery, ECHO transition signature, recording treatment, reveal silence and ending sound. Avoid constant loud music and cheap jumpscare stingers. Keep sound levels comfortable; offer mute/volume. Critical information must also be available visually. For third-party audio, require exact licence proof and attribution before import.
