# Player Experience Review

Do not read hidden implementation notes before the playtest if a blind test is requested. Record what you believe, what you notice, what you try, where you hesitate and what you infer after each beat. Separate intended uncertainty from accidental confusion. Check controls, readability, accessibility and emotional response. Report actionable findings with severity and reproduction steps.
