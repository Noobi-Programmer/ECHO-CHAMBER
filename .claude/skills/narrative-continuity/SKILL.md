# Narrative Continuity

Build a fact ledger and event timeline. For every recording, state when it was created, who created it, what that person knew and what they wanted the player to infer. Flag contradictions and classify as intentional, explainable or accidental. Trace every reveal to earlier evidence. Check character motivation and causal transitions. Do not repair canon silently; submit proposed fixes for approval.
