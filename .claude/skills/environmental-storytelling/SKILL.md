# Environmental Storytelling

For every room, define its dramatic purpose, before/after state, key object, clue, player action, inferred fact and visual focal point. Use the same prop to mean something different after the reveal. Do not rely only on readable logs. Ensure the player can notice clues at target resolution and can inspect important objects. Each environmental detail must support atmosphere, character, causality or puzzle logic.
